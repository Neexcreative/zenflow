# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Anyone who struggles with focus — students, remote workers, developers, creatives. They open Zenflow when they need to get into a flow state: studying for exams, deep work sessions, creative work, long coding sprints. They may have lo-fi streams and multiple apps open; Zenflow collapses that whole setup into a single, beautiful tab.

## Product Purpose

Zenflow is a browser-based focus and flow environment. It provides a Pomodoro timer, ambient sound library, YouTube music integration, clock, weather, notes, tasks, and atmospheric background themes — all in one calm, premium interface. Success looks like: the user opens it, chooses a background and sound, and disappears into their work for hours.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, premium. Like a beautifully designed room built for deep work. The kind of app you're proud to have open on your screen.

## Anti-references

Gamified productivity apps (Habitica, streaks, XP, badges, achievement toasts) — these feel juvenile and pull attention away from the work. Zenflow earns loyalty through experience, not reward loops.

## Design Principles

1. **Atmosphere over features** — Zenflow is a place you go to be in flow, not a feature checklist. Every UI decision should serve the ambient experience first.
2. **Premium without showoff** — The quality is self-evident; it doesn't need to shout. No achievement screens, no streaks, no confetti.
3. **Calm confidence** — The UI should feel like a quiet room with good lighting. Interactions are smooth and satisfying, never urgent or loud.
4. **Mood is the interface** — Background themes, ambient sounds, and visual transitions aren't decoration — they ARE the product. Treat them as first-class UX.
5. **Earned simplicity** — Complexity lives under the surface. New users see a minimal focus environment; power users find depth through exploration, not tutorial walls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: adequate color contrast, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` support for all animations. The ambient mesh and background animations already have a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` block — keep it exhaustive as features expand.
